# Music and sound

The songs and the sound effects are original. They are written in `src/audio/sound.js` and played by the browser. They are not copied from another game or from a recording. No extra license applies, and you do not owe anyone a fee to use them in this game.

## Songs

Two looping tunes. Music has its own volume, separate from sound effects. A tune keeps going while hits, moves, and menu taps play. It only changes when you go between the map and a battle, and that change fades across.

- **Voyage** plays on the title, the island map, and the menus. It is a bright major-key loop, about 104 beats a minute, built from soft chords, a light bass, and a short melody.
- **Clash** plays in battles, including the champion. It is a little quicker, about 120 beats a minute, still in a major key so it stays upbeat for ages 10 to 12.

Settings already saves a music slider, an effects slider, and a mute switch for each player. On a phone, music starts off until someone turns it up. Nothing plays until the first tap.

## Sound effects

Moves, hits, misses, status, catches, level-ups, evolutions, and menu taps are short synthesized notes. They use the effects volume, not the music volume.

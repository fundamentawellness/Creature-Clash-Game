# Tile art

The Greenridge map uses original pixel art drawn in `src/game/tileset.js`. The tiles, the walking character, and the townspeople are not copied from a tile pack, from Pokémon, or from any other game. No extra license applies.

The pictures are 16 by 16 pixels: grass, tall grass, forest, trees, path, sand, shore grass, water, walls, floors, doors, cave floor, cave wall, hedge, and boulder. The player is a small original figure in a blue shirt. Townspeople reuse that figure with a different color so each person is easy to tell apart.

Later islands can add their own tiles in the same file. Cut, Surf, and Rock Smash are not open yet. The hedge, the deep water, and the boulder on Greenridge are the spots those skills will use.
